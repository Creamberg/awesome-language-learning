# Hungarian (Magyar) learning resources

A short list of free materials for studying Hungarian. Where a free resource requires registration or has a limited scope, that is noted.

## Structured courses and archives

- [Practice Your Hungarian — Stipendium Hungaricum](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/practice-your-hungarian/): A free 22-module A1 self-study course covering everyday situations, study, work, travel and health. Registration is required; the course is aimed at international students in Hungary.
- [DLI Hungarian courses — Live Lingua](https://www.livelingua.com/project/dli/Hungarian): Free online and downloadable DLI Hungarian texts and audio, including a multi-volume basic course.

## Interactive lessons and recall practice

- [SziaMed — everyday and medical Hungarian](https://sziamed.com/try): Free guest lessons, listening, vocabulary and recall practice for everyday Hungarian and patient conversations. Guest use needs no account; guest progress is not saved. **Disclosure:** one maintainer contributing to this list operates SziaMed. SziaMed describes its patient cases as fictional and its voices as synthetic; its public site says independent Hungarian-language, pronunciation, level and medical review are pending.
